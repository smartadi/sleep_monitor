- **Added** `analysis/swa_validation/cap_arousal_events.py` — an **event inventory** for CAP transients that are not head movement, deliberately built as reporting rather than inference (no p-values, no AUC, no classifier). Detection on CLE−CRE at a 1 s grid: detrended std in a 2 s window (the detrend matters — a 2 s slice of the overnight drift is not an event), robust z against a centred 4 min rolling median/MAD, event = z ≥ 3 for ≥ 2 s, gaps < 5 s merged. Four rejection gates, each reported as a count: head motion (dynamic-acceleration z ≥ 3 within ±5 s), head turn (> 5° over ±10 s, using the corrected `motion.head_angle`), level step (CAP mean shifts > 5 robust sd across the event = electrode re-seat), impulse (single sample-to-sample jump > 20 robust sd = electrical glitch). Surviving events are sorted into four classes by what the OTHER channels show at onset: 1 scored arousal overlaps ±3 s; 2 no scored arousal but EEG 8–30 Hz rises (z ≥ 2); 3 neither, but HR surge ≥ 3 bpm (ECG R-peaks) and/or pulse-wave amplitude ≤ 0.70 of baseline; 4 nothing — reported, not hidden, as the error bar on classes 2 and 3. Thresholds are descriptive criteria and are printed with the summary so any number is traceable. Outputs `reports/swa_validation/cap_events/{cap_arousal_events,cap_arousal_summary,cap_arousal_by_stage}.csv`; figures `writeup/figures/cap_events/{event_inventory,event_profiles,event_examples_<S>,event_night_<S>}.png`. Run: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe analysis/swa_validation/cap_arousal_events.py --all`.

- **Rewrote** `analysis/slow_wave/harmonic_ladder_overlay.py` as a **flat harmonic-ladder episode detector**, following a conceptual reframe: the LADDER is a *distinct* phenomenon (a rich, temporally-flat harmonic comb episode — a candidate SWA signature), NOT the resp/cardiac harmonic series the ridge tracker already reports, so it is a single concept (no resp/cardiac split) and must be temporally flat/stable rather than a per-window feature. Per channel (kept separate — averaging diluted single-electrode episodes): (1) enhanced spectrogram (per-column frequency-background subtraction, dB); (2) per-window, find the RICHEST consecutive comb over an f0 grid (0.15–0.55 Hz) via rung significance = peak > `RUNG_DB=4` dB above local background — the count only, no committed f0, sidestepping HPS octave ambiguity; (3) **session-relative** richness gate — a window is candidate only if its comb is `RICH_MARGIN=3` rungs richer than that channel's baseline (a ~4-rung respiratory comb is present most of the night, so an absolute threshold flags everything); (4) bridge dropouts, block; (5) per block fit ONE f0 (max total rung support across the block) → inherently FLAT, resolves octave ambiguity by support; (6) **hysteresis** — grow the episode outward from the rich core while a basic comb (`EXTEND_RUNGS=4`) persists at that f0, so a ladder spans its full extent not just its peak; (7) draw each rung at constant k·f0 (flat horizontal lines), temporally persisted. Verified on S6N1: catches the 2.25–2.7 h and 3.5–4.05 h episodes (CH 43 min ×7 rungs f0=0.24, in N2) as flat ladders and stays quiet elsewhere. This supersedes the same-day earlier iterations (per-window comb-contrast, combined→per-channel) which were unstable (vertical noise streaks) and split resp/cardiac. Report CSV: `session,channel,f0_hz,median_rungs,active_min,longest_min,dominant_stage`. One figure/session (3 channel panels + stage ladder) → `writeup/figures/harmonics/ladders/`.
- **Refined** the same detector after the threshold-tuning proved to be whack-a-mole (each fix broke another session). Rather than tune, INSTRUMENTED the data on the visible S6N1 episodes to find the actually-discriminative feature: (a) tested a "principled" spectral-autocorrelation comb strength — it did NOT separate episode from baseline (episode z ≈ 0), because the comb is a small fraction of total spectral variance; (b) a dB sweep of the bright-rung count DID separate cleanly — at `RUNG_DB=5` (peak ≥5 dB above the local background) the episodes carry ~5 rungs vs baseline 0, while at 4 dB noise floods every window.
