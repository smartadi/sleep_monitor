## 2. Content highlights

**Delta bursts framed as an SWS phenomenon** (intro ¶21; the whole of methods
¶170). Section 3.6 reports that most detected onsets were *isolated N2 slow
waves and K-complexes*, because sustained N3 activity has no discrete onset —
so the intro and methods contradicted the result. Both now say delta bursts
occur throughout NREM and are *most sustained* during SWS, and the methods
rationale states the N2/K-complex origin up front instead of walking it back.

**"asymmetry" vs "imbalance"** (¶188, highlighted twice). The same quantity was
called asymmetry, asymmetric direction, and imbalance — sometimes inside one
paragraph — while the figures, the supplement and §4.5 call it *imbalance*.
Standardised on **imbalance** across §3.1, §4.5 and Figure S12.

**"Overall, SEC amplitude coupling was weak…"** (¶223). It was stranded after
the forward reference to §3.3 and repeated the conclusion of the paragraph
above. Folded into the sentence it qualifies; nothing dropped.

**"integer-spaced."** (¶338). The listed rungs — 0.15, 0.28, 0.42, 0.68,
0.95 Hz — are not consecutive multiples, because the 4th and 6th are missing,
so "approximately, but not exactly, integer-spaced" did not survive contact with
the numbers next to it. Now stated as the **1st, 2nd, 3rd, 5th and 7th multiples
of a 0.14 Hz fundamental, deviating by up to 9%**, with intermediate multiples
noted as often absent. (Least-squares fundamental over those five rungs is
0.1366 Hz; residuals +0.013, +0.007, +0.010, −0.003, −0.006 Hz.)

**"capacitive deflections per heartbeat"** (¶363). *k* is the ratio of *detected
peaks* to reference cycles — which is what the next sentence already reports
("2.02 peaks per cardiac cycle"). "Deflections" implied every deflection was
counted. Reworded in both bands.

**§4.5 heading + its whole body** (¶372–373). The Discussion had **two
Limitations sections, §4.4 and §4.6**, with §4.5 Comparison wedged between them.
Comparison moved ahead of the limitations and renumbered **§4.4**; the two
limitation blocks merge into a single **§4.5 Limitations** (the second block's
enumeration now announces itself: "Several further limitations apply. First, …").

**Table 3 row labels** (all four highlighted). The first ran `k(` together, the
second **had lost its *k* entirely** ("Same participant's other-night "), and
*k* was roman where the rest of the document sets it italic. All four labels
rewritten consistently.

**"mean (Figures 4 and S8)."** (¶299). Figure S8's own caption used to
self-reference ("drawn as in Figure S8"), fixed in the S1–S12 renumber. S8 is
the cardiac all-nights figure, so the citation is correct as it stands.
