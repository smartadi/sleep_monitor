# Review: CAP Sleep Mask Manuscript V18

Manuscript V18 + Supplementary V8 · Nov 6, 2026

## How to use this review

These are suggestions, not tracked changes: nothing in the .docx has been altered. Items are grouped by type and ordered roughly by how much a reviewer would care. Section numbers refer to the manuscript (V18) and figure numbers starting with S refer to the supplement (V8).

**If you only fix eight things, fix these:**

1. The headline claim of lower respiratory ridge power in N3 (Abstract, 3.3, Discussion, Conclusion) is never actually shown in Results (Major 1).
2. Several correlations treat the 12 nights as independent and are Pearson, while the supplement says Spearman with participant-level permutation (Major 2).
3. Section 3.9 uses features that are never defined in Methods (impulse frequency, SEC area, rms), and two different results share identical statistics (Major 3).
4. Eye movements are an obvious alternative explanation for the stage-dependent variance and event responses, and the PSG EOG needed to check this is already available (Major 4).
5. The arousal-exclusion results for spindles and delta bursts are described in Methods but not reported, and the Fig. 7 caption says most onsets were within 10 s of an arousal (Major 5).
6. The spectral-slope method as written leaves no frequencies to fit (Inconsistencies table).
7. The supplement's statistics section describes different tests and analyses from the main text (Major 10).
8. The conflict-of-interest statement says no author has a financial interest, but two authors are at Nanosync Labs (Section-by-section, Acknowledgement).

It could also contribute to the REM-onset slope result and to frontal-EEG event detection. *Suggestion:* use the PSG EOG channels to exclude epochs with high EOG activity (or regress EOG out) and show that the variance-by-stage and event-triggered results survive. Discuss this explicitly in the limitations.
