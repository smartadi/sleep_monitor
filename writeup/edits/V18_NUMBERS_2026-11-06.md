## R4 · Is the N3 drop in ridge power respiration-specific? → points 53, 2c, 79a

Count-matched, motion-free epochs (≥ 10 N3 epochs per night, 200 draws), participants with ridge power lower in N3 than in other stages (by mean / by median):

| | resp CH | resp CLE | resp CRE | card CH | card CLE | card CRE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ridge power (V18's measure) | 5/6 · 6/6 | 4/6 · 5/6 | 5/6 · 5/6 | 5/5 · 5/5 | 5/5 · 5/5 | 2/3 · 2/3 |
| total power in the band | 5/6 · 6/6 | 5/6 · 5/6 | 5/6 · 6/6 | 5/6 · 5/6 | 6/6 · 6/6 | 5/6 · 6/6 |
| **ridge power ÷ band power** | **2/6 · 2/6** | **2/6 · 1/6** | **4/6 · 4/6** | 5/5 · 4/5 | 4/5 · 3/5 | 2/3 · 2/3 |

**Result: the respiratory drop is not respiration-specific.** Total power in the respiratory band falls in N3 just as much; relative to it, the respiratory ridge is lower in N3 in only 2 of 6 participants on C_H and C_LE (4 of 6 on C_RE). The cardiac ridge keeps a relative drop on C_H (5 of 5) and partly on C_LE.

**Text for 53 (replaces the draft):** "After count-matching motion-free epochs, respiratory ridge power was lower in N3 than in other stages in 5 of 6 participants on C_H (6 of 6 by median) and in 4–5 of 6 on C_LE and C_RE. Total power in the respiratory band fell by a similar amount, however: relative to band power, the respiratory ridge was lower in N3 in only 2 of 6 participants on C_H and C_LE. The N3 reduction therefore reflects lower overall signal amplitude rather than a respiration-specific change. Cardiac ridge power remained lower in N3 relative to band power on C_H in all 5 testable participants."

**Knock-on:** the abstract's "lower respiratory ridge power in deep sleep" (2c) and the Discussion ¶1 sentence (79a) need the same caveat, or should move to "lower SEC power in N3".

## R6 · Do the PSG's own signals show the LFO ridges and combs? → points 71, 81d, 85, S16

All 12 nights had usable Pleth, Thorax, Abdomen and Flow (flat in < 4% of epochs).

**LFO ridges vs finger PPG.** The same low-band detector was run on the PSG finger PPG (Pleth) and on SEC C_RE.

- Share of the night with a ridge: SEC **0.12 (0.05–0.20)**, Pleth **0.11 (0.06–0.18)**.
- They rarely coincide. P(Pleth ridge | SEC ridge) has a median of **0.00**, against a Pleth base rate of 0.11; it is above chance on only **3 of 12** nights.
- Where both have a ridge, frequencies match within 0.01 Hz on 2 nights only (S1N2, S3N2).
- **Reading:** the SEC LFO ridges are not the vasomotion seen in finger PPG at the same time. That argues against a systemic oscillation that also shows at the periphery, but a local cranial source is not excluded. For 81d, the Mayer-wave/PPG framing is not supported by co-occurrence.

**Harmonic combs vs respiratory belts and flow.** The s06 comb score was computed on Thorax, Abdomen and Flow; for each of the 45 SEC comb events it was compared with 200 random NREM spans of the same length from the same night.

- Event vs null, median comb z: Thorax **0.57 vs 0.08**, Abdomen 0.52 vs 0.03, Flow 0.57 vs 0.08.
- Above the night's 95th-percentile null in only **12, 13 and 9 of 45** events respectively. Breathing is slightly more regular during comb events, as fits stable N2, but in most events the belts show no comb.
- **Comb bands at multiples of the breathing rate:** the share of an event's bands within 0.03 Hz of k × thorax breathing rate has a median of **0.20, against 0.24 expected by chance**; above chance in 11 of 45 events. Comb fundamental ÷ breathing rate: median 0.67 (IQR 0.65–0.91); within ±10% in 9 of 45.
- **Reading:** the combs are not respiratory harmonics. Text for 71: "Simultaneous thoracic and abdominal belts and nasal flow showed comb structure above their own random-NREM baseline in at most 13 of the 45 events, and the SEC comb bands did not fall at multiples of the breathing rate more often than chance (median 0.20 of bands vs 0.24 expected), so the events were not a harmonic image of respiration." Caveat: the belts are filtered at 1 Hz, so only harmonics below 1 Hz are tested.

**Methods point found on the way (new; goes with 70 / Methods P155).** In **29 of 71** channel episodes, the fitted comb fundamental sits within 0.012 Hz of the 0.15 Hz floor of the search range. For many events the true spacing may lie below the range, or the bands may not be harmonic at all. Either widen the search range, or describe the events as multi-band and drop "fundamental" from the text.

Tables: `paper/outputs/tables/s11_psg_origin/lfo_sec_vs_pleth.csv`, `comb_events_psg.csv`, `psg_channel_quality.csv`.
