**How they were made**
- **Size:** each figure is drawn at SLEEP print size: 178 mm two-column, 86 mm one-column, at least 300 dpi. SLEEP states no column width, so these are OUP's usual columns.
- **Type:** Arial, 7 pt labels and 6 pt ticks.
- **Checks:** Anna's `check_figure` reports 0 required issues on every file.
- **Numbers:** every stage's numbers file and `PAPER_NUMBERS.md` are byte-identical to before the pass.
- **Formats:** each figure has a `.png` (preview) and a `.pdf` with editable text, for Word or Illustrator.

**Not touched:** co-author figures (Fig. 1, Fig. 3 correlations, Fig. 4 spectra, S1, S5, S16), and your supplementary figures that the pipeline does not draw: S2, S3, S7, S10, S12, S14, S15, S17. Those could be ported next.

## Independent critique (a reviewer that saw only the PNGs and one-line claims)

Its points, condensed, with my view. These are reviewer risks, not styling problems.

1. **Fig. 8, K-complex latency.** The critic says the per-event latencies spread almost evenly over the 0–10 s search window. A median of a uniform 0–10 s window is 5 s, "so the 5.2 and 5.5 s medians are what noise would give". Panel c's peak is also positive by construction.
   - **I agree.** The null's latency and peak distributions belong on the figure, or the latency claim should rest on the grand average in panel a only.
   - **Decided 2026-11-07: grand average only.** The per-event panel is gone; peaks are 3.9 / 3.9 / 4.1 s (point 65c).
2. **Fig. 6, spindles.** Sigma is shown only pooled, for C_H. The typical spindle changes +0.13 dB (median), and 51.8% of spindles rise against a 50% chance level.
   - **I agree** this is weak support for the "low-frequency rises, sigma doesn't" claim at the single-spindle level.
   - Options: show sigma per recording, add a random-N2 control, or exclude spindles near delta bursts or K-complexes.
   - **Explainer (2026-11-07):** `s10_review_v18/explain_fig6_spindles`, four panels built from the existing s07 tables. Not a paper figure.
3. **Fig. 2, head turns.** The direction of the step flips between participants (a vs c), and panel d is weak.
   - **I agree.** The text should claim that steps coincide with head turns, not their direction.
   - The critic thinks the hypnogram is unnecessary; I disagree, because it is part of the V18 figure.
4. **Fig. 9, "harmonic".** The marked bands are not integer multiples of one fundamental.
   - **I agree.** This matches R6: bands sit at breathing-rate multiples only at chance level, and the fitted f0 sits at the search floor in 29 of 71 episodes (point 70).
5. **Fig. 10, reproducibility.** S2 drives the correlations, and "correlation is not reproducibility".
   - **I agree.** Report the ICC (0.69 / 0.86 / 0.40, numbers sheet R3) next to r. A leave-S2-out r was not computed.
6. **Eye-movement panel, REM.** Only 1 night keeps enough REM after the control.
   - **I agree, and I corrected** the R2 text in the numbers sheet and point 72a.
7. **Fig. 5, ridges.** The ridges are not visible under the tracker lines at print size, and there is no colorbar.
   - **Partly agree.** A thinner overlay or an un-overlaid inset would show the evidence. That goes beyond polish, so it's your call.
8. **Smaller points.**
   - Fig. 7 gives each panel its own y-scale, which hides that C_LE responds 2–3× less.
   - Fig. 11 treats nights as independent for PSQI; D2 handles this.
   - "Highest on C_H" fails for S4 in the SNR figure.
